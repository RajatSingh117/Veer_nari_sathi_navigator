## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a compassionate, solemn, and impeccably reliable civic companion. Built for widows (Veer Naris) and families of fallen Indian armed forces personnel, the aesthetic communicates unwavering respect, institutional reliability, and gentle guidance.

### Emotional Narrative & Tone
- **Dignity & Reverence:** The visual tone avoids commercial slickness, gamification, or celebratory noise. It honors service, sacrifice, and resilience through grounded layouts, calm proportions, and an absence of frantic visual cues.
- **Profound Reassurance:** Users are frequently elderly, grieving, navigating complex bureaucracy in rural or semi-urban settings, and unfamiliar with digital administration. The interface acts as a protective, transparent elder advisor—calm, patient, steady, and clear.
- **Civic Authority Without Hostility:** State authority is translated into accessible civic stewardship. Forms, statuses, and legal documentation requirements appear as step-by-step paths rather than daunting gatekeeping hurdles.

### Design Movement: Modern Civic Humanism
The aesthetic blends **Modern Institutional Clarity** with **High-Accessibility Humanism**:
- High-contrast, pure-toned surfaces anchored by deep naval discipline.
- Tactile, generous interactive components engineered specifically for tremors, low vision, and cognitive fatigue.
- Subdued national iconography woven with strict editorial reserve: national tricolor markers appear strictly as subtle, dignified grounding elements (thin accent dividers, trust micro-badges), never as decorative clutter.
- Ubiquitous, prominent multi-modal access with persistent voice readouts and large-type options integrated directly into foundational navigation.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around extreme legibility, dignified Indian administrative prestige, and WCAG AAA compliance (targeting 7:1 contrast on all operational text).

### Core Palette Roles
- **Primary Navy (`#1B2A41`):** Represents structural authority, steady discipline, and administrative endurance. Used for major mastheads, core action buttons, authoritative cards, and primary level headings.
- **Warm Saffron / Amber Gold (`#D97706`):** Evokes valor, selfless warmth, and vital attention. Reserved for contextual alerts, primary focus indicators, key status triggers, and active voice assistance affordances.
- **Tricolor Green (`#138808`):** Represents verified entitlements, cleared pension claims, and successful document verifications. Paired with subtle muted tints for high-visibility validation.
- **Neutral Deep Slate (`#0F172A`):** The foundational text ink. Delivers a guaranteed minimum contrast ratio of over 10:1 against white backgrounds, eliminating eye fatigue for aging eyes.

### Background & Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#F8F9FA`):** An ivory-tinted, anti-glare canvas that softens visual harshness while maintaining crisp spatial separation.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white container surfaces framing critical records, input groups, and application stages.
- **Border Structural Key (`#CBD5E1` & `#E2E8F0`):** Solid, intentional outlines defining interactive zones, preventing loss of boundary perception on low-grade mobile screens.

### Administrative & Status Semantics
- **Success / Entitlement Active (`#138808`):** Claims ready to disburse, verified identity certificates.
- **Pending / Action Required (`#D97706` / `#B45309`):** Missing military service books, pending family pensions, upcoming life certificate submission deadlines.
- **Processing / Official Verification (`#1E40AF`):** Defense Accounts Department processing, bank branch cross-checks.

## Typography

Typography in this design system prioritizes uncompromised visual clarity, immediate legibility for vernacular multi-script parity, and high legibility across modest mobile displays.

### Font Family Strategy
**Noto Sans** is designated across headlines, body, and UI labels. It delivers comprehensive typographic parity across Devanagari, Gurmukhi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, and other Indian scripts, ensuring seamless switching between English and regional languages without layout breakage or font-weight degradation.

### Typographic Principles
- **Elevated Floor Size:** The base reading text begins at a generous `18px` (`body-lg`) with an expansive line height of `30px` (~1.66). Small print under `14px` is strictly prohibited.
- **Sturdy Weights:** Body copy relies on Medium (`500`) weight rather than thin or light styles, preserving stroke contrast against anti-glare screens or low-brightness settings.
- **Headline Grounding:** Headers rely on clear SemiBold (`600`) and Bold (`700`) weights with deliberate, unhurried leading, ensuring elderly users never lose their reading place during multi-line guidance.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy centers on a relaxed, predictable single-track hierarchy designed to prevent cognitive overload.

### Structural Rhythm
- **Reading Column Anchor:** Multi-column layouts are avoided for critical legal, administrative, and pension workflows. Guidance sequences are restricted to a maximum width of `760px` centered on desktop screens, minimizing excessive lateral saccades for elderly eyes.
- **Rhythm & Safe Envelopes:** Outer page margins on mobile remain padded at a reliable `1rem` (16px), expanding to `2rem` (32px) on desktop to guarantee breathing room. Component interiors feature deep breathing space (`space-md` at 20px, `space-lg` at 32px), ensuring interactive elements never crowd neighboring touchpoints.

### Responsive Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 640px):** Single vertical column. Full-width touch targets. Persistent bottom utility bar housing the audio readout toggle and direct telephone nodal officer link.
- **Tablet (640px – 1024px):** Single-column workflow layout with side-pinned step progress ledger.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** Maximum canvas limit of `1200px`. Two-tier split: main transactional navigator (8 columns) alongside an authoritative administrative directory & helpline drawer (4 columns).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on crisp tactile separation rather than multi-layered skeuomorphism or floaty glassmorphism, which frequently create readability barriers for visually impaired or elderly individuals.

### Tonal Stratification
- **Ground Floor (L0):** Canvas base (`#F8F9FA`).
- **Functional Container (L1):** White surface (`#FFFFFF`) framed with a crisp `1.5px` border in `#E2E8F0`. All standard claim cards, guidance sections, and pension step trackers sit here.
- **Elevated Interactive Layer (L2):** Modals, document capture viewports, and floating voice assistance bars. These employ a deep, low-spread ambient drop: `0px 4px 16px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)` paired with an anchor border in `#CBD5E1`.

### High-Contrast State Anchoring
Depth transitions occur primarily through border enhancement and tone change rather than shadow expansion. When an interactive card or input field receives focus or active interaction, its outline weight transitions to a sturdy `2px` or `3px` solid stroke in Warm Saffron (`#D97706`) or Primary Navy (`#1B2A41`), guaranteeing unmistakable focus visibility.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an approachable, reassuring geometric language balancing civic stability and human warmth.

### Corner Radii
- **Level 2 (Standard Rounded):** Base interactive components, inputs, and status badges carry an `8px` (`0.5rem`) radius.
- **Containers & Document Shields (`rounded-lg`):** Cards, administrative panels, and document status sections employ a `16px` (`1rem`) radius, providing a soft, protective outline.
- **Audio Affordances & Quick Triggers (`rounded-full`):** Read-aloud speaker buttons, primary step chips, and emergency nodal contact buttons adopt full pill geometry (`9999px`), communicating their instant, tool-like nature.

### Restraint
No sharp `0px` harsh angles (which can feel intimidatingly legalistic) and no overly playful or fluid organic curves. Every corner communicates orderly, considered care.

## Components

Every component is constructed to exceed international accessibility standards, catering directly to elderly users, motor tremors, and grief-induced disorientation.

### 1. Primary Action & Voice "Listen" Buttons
- **Touch Targets:** Minimum height of `56px` across all form factors; mobile primary buttons span `64px` for foolproof thumb engagement.
- **Primary Civic Button:** Solid Navy background (`#1B2A41`), white text (`#FFFFFF`), `Noto Sans SemiBold 18px`, `8px` rounded corners. Hover state shifts to `#2A3F5F`; active focus ring is an explicit `3px` offset ring in Saffron Gold (`#D97706`).
- **Listen / Audio Assist Affordance:** Every section header, pension amount display, and legal instruction panel is paired with an inline or anchored pill button featuring an unmissable speaker icon and bold text: **"सुनें / Listen"**. Background is a gentle Saffron tint (`#FEF3C7`), border `1.5px` solid `#D97706`, and text in `#B45309`. Activating initiates a clear, human-paced spoken breakdown.

### 2. High-Contrast Status Chips
- Height of `36px` with bold text (`label-md`).
- **Ready to Apply / Verified:** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, left-border indicator `4px` solid `#138808`.
- **Action Needed / Missing Document:** Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, left-border indicator `4px` solid `#D97706`.
- **In Official Review:** Background `#EFF6FF`, text `#1E40AF`, left-border indicator `4px` solid `#2563EB`.

### 3. Accessible Form Controls & Inputs
- **Height & Font:** Form inputs maintain a minimum height of `56px` with `18px` typing font size to eliminate mobile zooming and eye strain.
- **Visual Structure:** White background, solid `2px` border in `#CBD5E1`. Floating labels are replaced by permanent, prominent top labels (`label-lg`) paired with explanatory secondary helper text in `16px` `#475569`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Scaled up to `24px × 24px` physical footprints inside generous `60px` height selectable rows with visible selection borders.

### 4. Document Verification & Extraction Cards
- Designed for reviewing Service Discharge Books, Pension Payment Orders (PPO), Death Certificates, and Sparsh migration notices.
- Features high-contrast ivory backgrounds with a designated thumbnail window, unambiguous validation status chip, and large key-value pairs (`label-md` keys, `body-xl` bold values).
- Includes a dedicated "Request Village Sahayak / Nodal Officer Visit" action on every card if documentation appears mismatched.

### 5. Civic Guidance Banner & Tricolor Grounding
- Key summary modules and authenticated pension entitlement screens feature a subtle, respectful top grounding divider: a precise `3px` hairline consisting of deep saffron, pure white, and sovereign green.
- Banners present an official disclaimer framing: authoritative, reassuring, stating official backing under Ministry of Defence and Directorate of Indian Army/Navy/Air Force Veterans, free from complex bureaucratic jargon.
