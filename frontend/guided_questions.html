Special Family Pension (SFP) Clarification

Under Regulation 213 of Pension Regulations for the Army (Part 1), the first nominated heir or legal widow retains primacy for continuous grant of Ordinary or Special Family Pension. If you have inquiries regarding disputed nomination or partition grants, your Zila Sainik Welfare Office will assign a dedicated welfare inspector.
